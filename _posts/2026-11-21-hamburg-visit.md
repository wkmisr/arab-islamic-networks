Wakako Kumakura, Erina Ota, Jun Ogawa and Toru Miura will visit Hamburg on 21 and 22 November 2026. The aim is to learn about the current state and plans of a research group that applies computational methods to Arabic-Islamic social history, and to explore ways of working together.

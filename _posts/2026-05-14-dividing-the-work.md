---
title: "Third meeting: dividing the work"
kind: "Project meeting"
summary: "How to encode more than 13,000 entries in four years: people first, everything else by machine, and work divided by the length of the entry."
---
Present: Erina Ota, Wakako Kumakura, Toru Miura. Three hours, on the update of the encoding application and on how to simplify the rules for the main phase of work.

To process more than 13,000 biographies within four years, the meeting agreed on a working principle. The checking and registration of **person identifiers**, above all for teachers, students and the subject himself, comes first and is done by hand. All other fields (subjects studied, institutions, places, *nisba*s, offices, family relations) are filled in by the machine in a first pass and refined later, once enough data has accumulated.

Work is divided by the length of the biography. Short entries, up to about 50 tokens, make up more than half of the text and go to research assistants. Medium entries of 50 to 100 tokens are taken up by team members. Longer entries are put off. The goal for this year is about 3,000 entries.

Frequent place, institution and office names were extracted from the whole text in a single run and registered in advance with provisional identifiers.

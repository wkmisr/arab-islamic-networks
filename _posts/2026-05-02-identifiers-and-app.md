---
title: "Meeting on identifiers and the encoding app"
kind: "Project meeting"
summary: "Rules for the shared identifier list, improvements to the encoding app, and conventions for entering names, places and dates."
---
Problems found during the first weeks of encoding were settled at this meeting.

**Identifier list**
- Gaps in the numbering are filled before new numbers are issued.
- GeoNames identifiers are written without a prefix.
- An ambiguous label such as *Ibn Fahd* receives a single identifier even if it may cover more than one person.
- The two pairs of holy cities are handled as two units: Mecca and Medina, and Jerusalem and Hebron.

**Encoding app**
- Method of study and field of study are recorded separately.
- New sections for biographical events and for social relations that are neither family nor teacher–student ties.
- Dates for events, with a certainty level, and a finer record of death dates (day, month, "about", "before", "after").
- A gender field, and an honorific option for titles, epithets and *kunya*.

**Conventions**
- The full name is recorded as far as the legal school, and in case of doubt a name element is kept rather than dropped.
- Posts held in two places are entered as two separate records.
- Collective terms such as "others" are not encoded.

Still open: whether to record a category for each person (scholar, soldier, merchant and so on), and whether to keep the biographical events section. A finer classification of *ijāza* was rejected.

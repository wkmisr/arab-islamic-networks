Online, 10:00 to 12:00 (JST). Jun Ogawa gave the opening report.

**Database.** As of 26 September there are 2,695 reviewed and 1,254 not yet reviewed XML files, 3,949 in all. When the entries of about 30 tokens are finished, work moves to entries of about 70 tokens and then about 130 tokens. To test whether the AI handles longer entries correctly, each member will prepare one reference encoding for each length.

**Method.** For the sake of efficiency and accuracy, the meeting suggested that the AI should no longer be asked to look up external reference identifiers.

**Plans.** A research visit to Hamburg is planned for 21 and 22 November. One in-person meeting will be held between January and March. Next year the team plans about three meetings, together with invited speakers.
